Create New Account

Creating a new Account is easy, just follow the instructions below!

  1. Username:
    Each MyJournal user must have their own unique username. Your username is what appears in the address to your journal and what you use to log into the MyJournal server. It also shows up when you post comments in other people's journal.
    Your username may contain only lower-case letters (a-z), digits (0-9) and the underscore character (_).
    Additionally, it must not be longer than 15 characters.
